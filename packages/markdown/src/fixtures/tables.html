Comparing night trains from Zurich

Four night trains leave Zurich for somewhere worth waking up in. They differ more in what a bed costs than in how long they take, and the cheapest bed is not always on the slowest train. The table below is the whole of the comparison; the paragraphs after it are the caveats.

Destination Leaves Arrives Couchette from
Hamburg 19:59 08:09 CHF 79
Vienna 21:40 07:49 CHF 69
Prague 21:40 10:34 CHF 89
Amsterdam 19:59 09:44 CHF 99 | sleeper only

Prices are the lowest the operators listed for a booking a month ahead. A couchette shares a compartment with five others; a sleeper costs about twice as much and shares with one.

The Vienna and Prague trains run as one as far as Linz, so a delay to one is a delay to both. Book the Hamburg train early in summer: it is the first to sell out.
