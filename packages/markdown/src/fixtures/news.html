Lake Zurich warms a full degree in a decade

By Anna Keller, 3 October 2026

The surface of Lake Zurich was on average a full degree warmer this summer than it was ten years ago, the cantonal water office said on Friday, the steepest rise it has measured since its records began in 1936.

Warmer water holds less oxygen, and the office warned that the deep water, which mixes with the surface only in a cold winter, has not turned over completely since 2019. Fish that live in the cold layer, the whitefish among them, are the first to feel it.

"A lake does not forget a warm winter," said the office's head of monitoring. The next report, due in spring, will say whether the past winter was cold enough to help.

The city plans to plant shade trees along the shore and to move two swimming areas away from the outflows that run warmest in August.
